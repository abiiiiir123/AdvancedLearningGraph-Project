About the similarity score
DistilBERT is a pre-trained language model, not a sentence-similarity model: it was never trained to compare sentences. The score is the cosine between centered mean-pooled embeddings (the corpus mean vector is subtracted to remove the shared component). Read the scores as a ranking (higher = semantically closer), not as calibrated probabilities. Green chips are skills that appear in both your query and the ad.
